SERVICES AGREEMENT (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)

This agreement is between Northwind Retail Pvt Ltd ("Client") and the freelancer named in the statement of work ("Freelancer").

1. Scope. Freelancer will provide UI design services as described in the statement of work.

2. Payment. Client will pay each invoice within 30 days of Client's written acceptance of the deliverable. Client may withhold acceptance at its sole discretion.

3. Ownership of work. All work product, including all deliverables, designs and any materials created in connection with this agreement, vests in Client immediately upon creation.

4. Pre-existing materials. Freelancer's templates and icon libraries may be incorporated into deliverables, and Client shall own all such materials upon incorporation.

5. Licence. Client receives a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide licence to use the deliverables in any medium.

6. Moral rights. Freelancer waives all moral rights in the deliverables.

7. Liability. Freelancer's liability for any claim arising under this agreement shall be unlimited.

8. Confidentiality. Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential for three years after termination.

9. Non-solicitation. Neither party will solicit the other's employees for twelve months after termination.

10. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of India. Courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
