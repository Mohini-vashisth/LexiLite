Checks a freelancer contract for intellectual-property clauses, and tells you what to ask the client to change.

Checked: IP assignment clauses only. Not checked: payment, liability, termination, confidentiality and everything else.

What happens to your contract
  • Your contract is read in memory and the result is returned. Nothing is saved unless you tick "Save this check".
  • A saved check stores the clause text as plain text for now. Encryption at rest is not built yet.
  • Saved checks expire after 30 days, and you can delete any of them at any time.
  • Whoever runs the server can read stored text. Your contract is not sent to any outside AI service.
